A little bit about us

Across time zones, into forever.

Two strangers, two cities, and one unexpected beginning.

Ours began the old-fashioned way, with a word from the families, and continued the new-fashioned way, across two cities and a time difference, in calls and countless conversations that were meant to be short and never were.

They say distance makes the heart grow fonder. For us, every mile apart only made us long a little more for the day we’d finally meet, even as we knew, quietly and surely, that we’d already chosen each other.

The celebrations

Two days in Ajabgarh

There will be love. There will be dancing. There will be a hillside, a great deal of food, probably some happy tears, and definitely tequila. We’re so grateful you’ll be with us as this next chapter begins.

Colour, custom, and a long evening of music.

Haldi & Mehendi

An afternoon of flowers by the water, with just a little turmeric. Henna, a long lunch, and someone will end up in the pool.

What to wear

Dress for the colours of the days

A guide, not a rulebook. Please don’t take any of it too seriously: as long as you feel beautiful, you’re good to go.

Love, Kansha

Haldi & Mehendi

A burst of pastels, or floral fusion. Turmeric does not come out, so nothing precious.

Sangeet

Light evening glamour in contemporary Indian. Embroidery, shimmer, things that move when you dance.

Bhaat & Gharva

Festive, in warm colours inspired by marigold and spices.

The wedding

Ethnic and regal elegance. Sarees, lehengas, sherwanis, bandhgalas. Please leave bridal red for the bride.

January in Rajasthan is sunny by day and genuinely cold once the sun sets, often around 7°C. Bring a shawl or a jacket for the evenings, and shoes that can manage lawns.

The venue

Ananta Spa & ResortAjabgarh, Rajasthan

Thirty acres of lawns and villas in a valley of the Aravalli foothills, about two hours’ drive from Jaipur, with the ramparts of Ajabgarh Fort on the ridge above and Bhangarh a short drive away. Both days take place within the resort.

Between celebrations there’s a long pool deck, the Tattva spa, and mustard fields to walk through at golden hour.
